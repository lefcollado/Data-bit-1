Photo: Florian Schmidt

Emergency vehicles in Germany are known for their brain-breaking sirens.
Their loud and piercing sounds decidely make room for the necessary service to get through -- it's impossible not to notice them.

Did you ever wonder where the fire trucks are going? Where in Berlin are all these fires? Are they really all fires?

Follow us in this interactive path to understand some of the fire brigade activity in Berlin near your home, work, school or any other place of interest.

About the data

This interactive path was developed in collaboration with HTW Berlin's Prof. Dr. Florian Koch using data from the Berliner Feuerwehr available in their official website as well as in their GitHub page. For more information and insights, check out their pages. Data on Berlin's population was gathered from the Amt für Statistik Berlin-Brandenburg's official page.
